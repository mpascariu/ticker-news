# GPW:PZU - 🗒 PZU Group Material News & Developments

▲ [Aug 28, 2026]: KNF clears PZU–Link4 merger, removing the last regulatory hurdle
│ 🔴 (high)         Poland's financial market supervisor KNF announced it found no grounds to object to the
│                   planned merger of PZU SA and Link4 TU, clearing the way for legal integration of the
│                   motor-insurance subsidiary; registration of the combined entity remains targeted for
│                   Q1 2027 under the group's multibrand strategy.
│                   Source:
[Bankier.pl](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/KNF-stwierdzila-brak-podstaw-do-zgloszenia-sprzeciwu-wobec-planowanego-polaczenia-PZU-z-Link4-9188748.html)
│
├─ [Aug 20, 2026]: Solvency II revision called neutral for PZU's capital and dividend policy
│ 🟡 (med)          In its H1 2026 results materials, PZU told investors that the forthcoming 2027 revision of
│                   the EU Solvency II directive will have a neutral impact on its ability to continue its
│                   capital and dividend policy — a reassurance for the record PLN 4.80/share payout and
│                   future distributions.
│                   Source:
[Bankier.pl](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Rewizja-Solvency-II-neutralna-dla-zdolnosci-PZU-do-kontynuowania-polityki-kapitalowo-dywidendowej-9184708.html)
│
├─ [Aug 20, 2026]: Q2 2026 net profit of PLN 1,647m beats consensus; H1 profit above PLN 3bn
│ 🔴 (high)         Q2 2026 net profit attributable to the parent rose to PLN 1,647m from PLN 1,470m a year
│                   earlier (+12% y/y) and came in about 7.2% above the analyst consensus of PLN 1,536m.
│                   H1 2026 group net profit exceeded PLN 3bn (down 6.8% y/y on weaker banking), gross
│                   insurance revenue rose PLN 426m y/y to PLN 15.7bn, life premiums grew 27% y/y and the
│                   Solvency II ratio stood at about 230%.
│                   Source: [Bankier.pl](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Zysk-netto-grupy-PZU-w-II-kw-26-wyniosl-1-65-mld-zl-powyzej-oczekiwan-9184674.html)
│
├─ [Aug 20, 2026]: PZU plans broad group-wide Personal Investment Accounts (OKI) offering
│ 🟢 (low)          CEO Bogdan Benczak said PZU intends to leverage the potential of the entire group,
│                   including its banks, to build a wide range of products under Poland's new Personal
│                   Investment Accounts (OKI) scheme; the insurer is also preparing a new claims-settlement
│                   system, with work on a partial internal model already well advanced.
│                   Source: [Bankier.pl](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/PZU-chce-wykorzystac-potencjal-calej-grupy-w-oferowaniu-produktow-OKI-9184915.html)
│
├─ [Aug 19, 2026]: Supervisory Board appoints PFR chief Piotr Matczuk to the Management Board
│ 🟡 (med)          PZU's Supervisory Board appointed Piotr Matczuk — until now CEO of state development
│                   fund PFR — as a Management Board member responsible for the investments area,
│                   effective September 1, 2026, with a term covering financial years 2027–2029.
│                   Source: [PZU Press Office](https://media.pzu.pl/pl/informacje-prasowe/szczegoly/piotr-matczuk-nowym-czlonkiem-zarzadu-pzu-sa)
│
└─ [Aug 3, 2026]: TFI PZU launches gold ETFs, extending passive lineup
  🟢 (low)          Group asset-management arm TFI PZU launched new exchange-traded funds offering retail
